# 🎮 GameHub

> **Game discovery made simple.**  
> Explore, track, and share games you’ll love.

## ✨ Overview
GameHub is an open-source platform focused on **game discovery**.  
Whether you’re a casual player hunting for hidden gems or a hardcore gamer curating your next marathon, GameHub helps you find and share the best games across platforms.

## 🚀 Features
- 🔍 **Smart Search & Filters** – Find games by genre, platform, tags, and release date.  
- 📝 **Game Details** – Screenshots, ratings, and community reviews at a glance.  
- ❤️ **Personal Collections** – Save favorites or create custom lists.  
- 🔗 **Cross-Platform Support** – PC, console, and mobile.
